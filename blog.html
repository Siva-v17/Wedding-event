Ask a planner

Questions our readers actually send us

How many months before the wedding should we book our venue?

For anywhere with a peak season — and most of India has one — aim for 10 to 12 months out. Popular Kerala backwater and Coorg estate venues get booked out that far ahead for the November–February window especially.

Asked by a reader planning a December wedding

Is it rude to ask for a specific gift instead of cash envelopes?

Not at all, as long as it's optional and never printed directly on the invitation. A simple mention on your wedding website, or a note passed along by family, keeps it polite and easy to ignore for anyone who'd rather not.

Asked by a reader planning a spring wedding

What should we actually budget for florals?

A good rule of thumb is 8–10% of the total budget, though it climbs quickly if you're covering a mandap or a large stage. Seasonal, locally grown blooms cut costs the most without anyone noticing the difference in photos.
