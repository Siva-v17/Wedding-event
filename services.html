Questions

Frequently asked

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book full planning 10–14 months out, though we've pulled off weddings in under 12 weeks. Earlier gets you first pick of venues and vendors.

Do you work with venues outside Chennai?

Yes — we regularly plan across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Kerala, plus destination weddings in Goa, Coorg and internationally.

Can we book just décor or just catering?

Absolutely. Every service on this page can be booked individually or bundled into a custom package.

What's included in the planning fee?

Our fee covers planning labour only — venue, catering and vendor costs are billed separately and transparently through your dashboard.
