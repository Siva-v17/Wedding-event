Before you enquire

A few quick answers

How much does a Stackly wedding cost?

Day-of coordination starts around ₹85,000; full planning starts around ₹4.5L. Your first call is free and we'll scope a number specific to your guest count and city.

Do you only work with couples in Chennai?

No — we plan across South India regularly and take on destination weddings anywhere, including outside India.

How soon can you start?

We can begin as soon as your consultation call is done. For dates under 12 weeks out, ask about our express planning track.
