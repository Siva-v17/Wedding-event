How we started

Built by a planner who got tired of chaos

Stackly Weddings began in 2012 as a one-woman coordination service for friends-of-friends. Word travelled faster than expected, and what started as weekend favours became a full studio with designers, coordinators and a vendor network spanning fourteen cities.

2012

First wedding coordinated on a spreadsheet and a prayer.

2016

Studio opens in Chennai; in-house décor and styling team formed.

2020

Destination weddings desk launched to serve couples abroad.

2026

320+ weddings produced across 14 cities and counting.
